Privacy Policy — Arrow Puzzle - Escape Flow

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Arrow Puzzle - Escape Flow ("we", "our", or "the app") is a casual puzzle game. We respect your privacy and do not collect personal information from players ourselves.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, star count, coin balance, settings, achievements and daily-streak information are stored only on your device using the browser's local storage. This information never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads). AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, and usage signals in order to serve and measure ads. See Google's privacy information at https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings.

Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send occasional reminders (daily challenge, streak at risk, achievements, events). You can disable notifications at any time from the system settings for the app.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are requested in a manner appropriate for a general audience.

Your choices

You may clear all locally saved data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
